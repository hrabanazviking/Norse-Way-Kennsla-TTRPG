# The Norse Way — Rule-Explainer Video Series

Cute ~30-second videos of **Unnr** explaining the homebrew rules of *The Norse Way* —
Volmarr's Viking-culture D&D system where **living the values is the winning move**.

Each episode teaches one real mechanic from
`lore/HOMEBREW_CODEX.md` (the Sagnaskemma storehouse): the history behind it,
the playable rule, and what the player learns. Gaming is how humans learn;
storytelling is how knowledge is taught. These videos are both at once.

## Format (Volmarr-approved)

- ~30 seconds, vertical **1080×1920**, MP4 (H.264 + AAC)
- **Rumi voice** narration (Unnr's official voice)
- Three scenes: Unnr presenting in the mead-hall → concept visuals → Unnr closing with a toast
- Narration laid over the full video; visuals outlast the voice (no clipped endings)
- No horned helmets, no modern flags, no Christian symbols in sacred scenes — canon law

## Written Rule Expansions

`rules/` holds the full written form of every episode's mechanic — one Markdown
file per episode (`rules/ep01-ordstir.md` … `rules/ep30-ship-culture.md`),
each in Volmarr's three-beat law:

1. **Grounding** — historical, mythological, or textual roots with real
   citations from the Mímir-Vörðr corpus; homebrew inventions labeled as such.
2. **The Complete Playable Rule** — full mechanics for D&D 5E *and* Old School
   Essentials, expanded beyond the video: edge cases, examples of play, every
   number a table needs.
3. **Teaching Beat** — what players learn about Viking-Age culture by playing it.

Provenance is labeled per section: Volmarr's canon is law; assistant additions
are marked. Mechanical source of truth: `HOMEBREW_CODEX.md`; dual-system
reference: `SYSTEM_VARIANTS.md`. Episodes 31+ get their rule files as their
video batch publishes.

**The master book:** `THE_NORSE_WAY_COMPLETE_RULES.md` gathers all 30 written
expansions into a single document — one H1, a linked table of contents, every
chapter demoted beneath it. The whole ruleset, cover to cover.
