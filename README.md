# Norse-Way-Kennsla-TTRPG
Homebrew TTRPG addon material to turn D&amp;D 5E, Old School Essentials, and other TTRPG systems into a true Viking setting based on authentic lore, culture, mythology, Norse-Paganism, and historical based ancient world value systems of the North. Low fantasy, history inspired, authentic Viking folklore and Norse mythology centered gameplay questing, centered on the human world of the ancestors and their own stories and imagination.
