# The Norse Way — Rule-Explainer Video Series

Cute ~30-second videos of **Unnr** explaining the homebrew rules of *The Norse Way* —
Volmarr's Viking-culture D&D system where **living the values is the winning move**.

Each episode teaches one real mechanic from
`lore/HOMEBREW_CODEX.md` (the Sagnaskemma storehouse): the history behind it,
the playable rule, and what the player learns. Gaming is how humans learn;
storytelling is how knowledge is taught. These videos are both at once.

## Format (Volmarr-approved)

- ~30 seconds, vertical **1080×1920**, MP4 (H.264 + AAC)
- **Rumi voice** narration (Unnr's official voice)
- Three scenes: Unnr presenting in the mead-hall → concept visuals → Unnr closing with a toast
- Narration laid over the full video; visuals outlast the voice (no clipped endings)
- No horned helmets, no modern flags, no Christian symbols in sacred scenes — canon law
