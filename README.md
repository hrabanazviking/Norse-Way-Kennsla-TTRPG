# Norse-Way-Kennsla-TTRPG
Homebrew TTRPG addon material to turn D&amp;D 5E, Old School Essentials, and other TTRPG systems into a true Viking setting based on authentic lore, culture, mythology, Norse-Paganism, and historical based ancient world value systems of the North. Low fantasy, history inspired, authentic Viking folklore and Norse mythology centered gameplay questing, centered on the human world of the ancestors and their own stories and imagination.

---

## Rule-Explainer Videos

Cute ~30-second vertical videos of **Unnr** explaining the homebrew rules of
*The Norse Way* — Volmarr's Viking-culture D&D system where **living the
values is the winning move**. Each episode teaches one real mechanic: the
history behind it, the playable rule, and what the player learns.

Format: ~30 seconds, vertical **1080x1920** MP4, Rumi-voice narration,
three scenes (Unnr presenting in the mead-hall, concept visuals, Unnr
closing). No horned helmets, no modern flags, no Christian symbols in
sacred scenes — canon law.
