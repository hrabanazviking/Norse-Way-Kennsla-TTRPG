## Project Vision

A complete historical low-fantasy sourcebook integrating 25 existing social/spiritual systems with expanded character options, equipment, bestiary, and campaign frameworks—dual-statted for D&D 5E SRD and Old School Essentials. Clean-room extraction from historical AD&D 2E Viking supplements, rewritten with modern scholarship and authentic cultural depth.

**Historical Scope:** Viking Age 793-1066 CE (Lindisfarne to Stamford Bridge)

## Mechanical Standards

### Three-Beat Structure (Every Section)

1. **Grounding:** Historical/mythological roots with citations from Mímir-Vörðr corpus
2. **The Complete Playable Rule:** Full mechanics for 5E AND OSE, edge cases, examples
3. **Teaching Beat:** What players learn about Viking culture through play

### Dual-System Formula

| Concept | 5E Implementation | OSE Implementation |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Core Mechanic | Proficiency bonus, advantage/disadvantage | d6 skill system, morale checks |
| DC/Resolution | DC 10-20 scale | 2d6 roll vs. target (6+ to 12+) |
| Scaling | Bounded accuracy (proficiency + stat) | HD-based progression |
| Social Conflict | Skill contests (CHA-based) | Reaction rolls modified by CHA |

**Conversion Reference:**
- 5E DC 10 ≈ OSE 2d6 vs. 6+
- 5E DC 15 ≈ OSE 2d6 vs. 9+
- 5E DC 20 ≈ OSE 2d6 vs. 12+
- 5E Advantage ≈ OSE roll 3d6 drop lowest
- 5E Disadvantage ≈ OSE roll 3d6 drop highest

### Provenance Marking

- **Volmarr's canon:** Unmarked, authoritative
- **Caducea additions:** Marked `[CH: addition]`
- **Historical facts:** Cited to source (saga, archaeology, scholarship)
- **Homebrew inventions:** Explicitly labeled `[Homebrew: invention]`

## Clean-Room Extraction Protocol

### Phase 1: Fact Mining
1. Document **only** factual claims from AD&D 2E supplement:
   - Historical dates, places, figures
   - Ship dimensions, weapon weights
   - Social structures, caste systems
   - Trade goods, economic data
2. Separate from **fictional content** (monsters, magic items, unique NPCs)
3. Cite to primary sources where possible

### Phase 2: Gap Analysis
1. Compare extracted facts against:
   - Existing 25 systems
   - Modern archaeological scholarship
   - Saga literature
2. Identify coverage gaps

### Phase 3: Original Composition
1. Write all content fresh using three-beat structure
2. Use extracted facts as historical anchors
3. Build new mechanics where old supplement had none
4. Explicitly mark all inventions as homebrew
