Unnr: The Norse year turns on two hinges. Winter: the sea closes, men come indoors — craft, feast, hold the Thing, pay their debts, learn the old arts. Summer: the sea opens — víking, trade, exploration. Raid in winter and you drown; never farm and you go hungry. The Viking Age was not pirates forever — it was farmers who sailed in summer, and every raid was weighed against the harvest left untended. Play the year the way a farmer's body knows it.
