Unnr: The Thing is law without kings — the assembly where free men settle blood-debts before witnesses. Four phases: the charge is announced, both parties swear with oath-helpers, the pleading begins — Insight to catch lies, Persuasion to move the crowd — and the law-speaker pronounces judgment: weregild, reconciliation, or outlawry. Full outlawry means any man may kill you, none may shelter you, and your name is struck from the community's frith. Law was procedure, and procedure was theater.
