Unnr: Flyting is war with words — formal, witnessed insult-dueling where wit and genealogy are the weapons. Best of three rounds: each exchange, you must speak a real insult aloud, in character, and every round it must cut deeper. Loki's rule: strike the live nerves — cowardice, kin-shame, ergi. The loser answers with holmgang or loses honor publicly. And if the níð-word goes unanswered by dusk, the accused is níðingr until blood or law answers it. Discipline your tongue like your sword-arm.
