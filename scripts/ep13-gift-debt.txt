Unnr: In the Norse world, money is memory. Every gift of real value creates a debt-token — the giver's claim on you, physical at the table, visible like honor. You must return something greater before the next seasonal turn, or lose Orðstír for every unanswered token. And weregild — the man-price — is the gift that ends a feud instead of starting a friendship: gold that covers the wound. Wealth hoarded is wealth wasted. Every transaction is a relationship.
