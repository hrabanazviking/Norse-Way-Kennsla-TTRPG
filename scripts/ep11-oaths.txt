Unnr: An oath is spoken aloud, in exact words, before witnesses — naming the god, the ancestors, the sword, or the ring you swear by. Vague oaths bind vaguely. While you chase a sworn oath, the cosmos lends you strength: advantage on one check a day that serves it. Break it, and you are eiðrofi — oath-breaker. Your Orðstír drops to one, and the old oath will be thrown back in your face for years. Fear your own mouth. Words were actions.
