# The Norse Way — System Variants: D&D 5E & Old School Essentials

Every mechanic in *The Norse Way* is written **system-first, setting-always**:
the culture, the history, and the teaching beat are identical at every table.
Only the dice change. This document gives both stat blocks side by side.

The OSE conversions follow Volmarr's own conversion law from
[RuneForgeAI-Norse-Saga-OSR](https://github.com/hrabanazviking/RuneForgeAI-Norse-Saga-OSR)
(*"HR1 determines what exists. OSE determines how it works."*)

## General conversion principles

| 5E concept | OSE equivalent |
|---|---|
| Ability check (d20 + mod vs DC) | Roll **1d20 ≤ ability score**; difficulty shifts the effective score ±1 to ±4 |
| Advantage / disadvantage | **±2** on the roll (or roll twice, keep better/worse, if the table prefers) |
| Saving throw vs DC | Nearest OSE save: Death, Wands, Paralysis, Breath, **Spells** |
| Charisma (Persuasion) checks | **2d6 reaction roll** — OSE's native reputation mechanic |
| DC 13 Wisdom save | **Save vs. Spells** |
| Alignment (law–chaos axis) | OSE's **Lawful / Neutral / Chaotic** — no conversion needed |

## Episode 1 — Orðstír (Honor as Spendable Currency)

*Orðstír 0–20; all PCs begin at 5; tracked publicly; +1 for open deeds
before witnesses; −1 to −3 for cowardice, oathbreaking, theft, unanswered
insult, miserliness; at 0, níðingr-adjacent; dying well passes half to
your heir.*

| | D&D 5E | OSE |
|---|---|---|
| **Spend honor** | 1-for-1 bonus on Charisma checks where your name matters | 1 point → **+1 on a 2d6 reaction roll** where your name matters, or **−2 on a roll-under CHA check** |
| **Everything else** | Identical | Identical — honor is honor in any century |

## Episode 2 — Frith (The Eight Circles of Sacred Peace)

*Breaking frith within a circle is a cosmic crime, answered with weregild,
outlawry, or feud.*

| | D&D 5E | OSE |
|---|---|---|
| **Breaking frith** | Disadvantage on social checks within the offended circle until weregild is paid | **−2 on reaction rolls** within the offended circle until weregild is paid |
| **Weregild & fines** | Paid in gp | Paid in gp — the OSE economy already speaks this language |
| **Outlawry** | As codex §XXII | As codex §XXII; additionally no hirelings will serve you and no stronghold will take you in |

## Episode 3 — Hamingja (Luck That Lives)

*Living 0–10 statistic; grows through courage, generosity, oath-keeping;
flees cowardice and shame; can be bequeathed.*

| | D&D 5E | OSE |
|---|---|---|
| **Spend luck** | 1 point → reroll a d20, take the better; or force a foe to reroll | **Identical** — rerolls are system-agnostic; 1 point → reroll any d20, take the better, or force a foe to reroll |
| **Everything else** | Identical | Identical |

## Episode 4 — Sumbel (The Vow-Cup)

*Minni-toasts and bragarfull vows; keeping the vow: +2 Orðstír, +1 Hamingja;
breaking it: oathbreaker penalties doubled.*

| | D&D 5E | OSE |
|---|---|---|
| **Vow kept** | +2 Orðstír, +1 Hamingja | Identical |
| **Vow broken** | Oathbreaker penalties (disadvantage on social checks among witnesses, −2 Orðstír, níð-mark) | Oathbreaker penalties (**−2 on reaction rolls** among witnesses, −2 Orðstír, níð-mark) |

## Episode 5 — Níð & the Níðingr (Shame as a Weapon)

*Three níð-marks = níðingr: oath worthless, killable without weregild,
Hamingja capped at 3.*

| | D&D 5E | OSE |
|---|---|---|
| **Marked (1–2 níð)** | Disadvantage on Persuasion/Deception with those who know | **−2 per mark on reaction rolls** with those who know |
| **Níðingr (3 marks)** | Oath worthless; killable without weregild; Hamingja capped at 3 | Identical; additionally retainers and men-at-arms abandon you, and hirelings cost double — if any will take your coin at all |

## Appendix — The Stranger's Reception (Codex §XXXIII)

*For the coming episode. A revealed non-human among humans triggers the
1d6 reception table (panic → holy dread); calm acceptance does not exist.*

| | D&D 5E | OSE |
|---|---|---|
| **Reception roll** | 1d6 per group/notable individual; extremity score = class + alignment weights | **Identical** — the table is system-neutral |
| **Iron-minded** (+1) | Fighter, Barbarian, Rogue, Ranger, Monk | Fighter, **Barbarian**, **Thief**, **Ranger** (OSE Advanced Fantasy) |
| **Spirit-touched** (−1) | Cleric, Druid, Wizard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Bard | Cleric, **Druid**, **Magic-User**, **Illusionist**, **Bard** (OSE Advanced Fantasy) |
| **Lawful** (+2) / **Chaotic** (−1) | As codex | **Identical** — OSE's Lawful/Neutral/Chaotic needs no conversion |
| **Ongoing (revealed)** | Disadvantage on Persuasion/Deception; advantage on Intimidation; no Orðstír earned while known | **−2 on reaction rolls** when parleying; **+2 on reaction rolls** when threatening; no Orðstír earned while known |
| **Wary respect** | DC 13 Wisdom save (spirit-touched) | **Save vs. Spells** (spirit-touched) |
