## The Rule (from HOMEBREW_CODEX.md §IX)

- An oath is sworn **aloud, in specific words, before witnesses**, naming the power sworn by (a god, one's ancestors, one's weapon, the oath-ring on the altar). Vague oaths bind vaguely — the GM holds the player to the **letter**.
- **Swearing grants power:** while pursuing a sworn oath, the PC has **advantage on one check per day** directly serving it — the oath *lends strength*, because the community and the cosmos are watching.
- **Breaking an oath:** the breaker becomes *eiðrofi* — Orðstír drops to **1 immediately**, all debt-tokens owed *to* them are void, and they gain **3 Wyrd-debt** (§III). Only a great public atonement (weregild paid triple, or a deed of saga-weight) can begin restoration — and the table *remembers*; NPCs will speak the old oath back at them for years.
- **The braga-goblet:** boasts sworn over the cup at sumbel (§IV) are oaths. The GM writes them down. The table *will* hold you to them.

## Teaching Beat

The player learns to *fear their own mouth* — to weigh words before speaking them, because words were actions. Modern casual promising dies at this table.
