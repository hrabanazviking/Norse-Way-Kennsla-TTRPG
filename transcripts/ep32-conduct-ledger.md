## The Rule (from HOMEBREW_CODEX.md §XXXII)

- *For the GM's screen.* Every behavior below moves the three purses — **Orðstír** (§I), **Hamingja** (§XVI), **Wyrd-debt** (§III) — plus níð-marks (§XVII) and the heavier sentences. Read it at the table's start; apply it aloud, with witnesses — because the culture did everything aloud, with witnesses.
- **Rewards** (acting the true Viking): courage in battle before witnesses **+1/+1**; an oath kept at real cost **+1/+1**; a fulfilled sumbel-vow **+2 Orðstír, +1 Hamingja**; a generous gift given openly **+1/+1**; lavish hospitality **+1**; holmgang victory **+1**; a drápa composed for another **+1 Orðstír (subject +1 too)**; feud settled at the Thing **+1 Orðstír both houses**; redeeming lost óðal **+3**; defending the óðal **+2/+1**; heeding a true dream **+1 Hamingja**; honoring the landvættir on new land **+1 Hamingja**; tending the ancestors' mound, season kept, **+1 Hamingja (household)**; answering a kin-claim **+1 Orðstír and the kin owes a favor**; refusing baptism under pressure **+1 (old-faith), +1 Hamingja**; feud-settling marriage **+2 both houses**; dying well — half Orðstír inherited, plus saga-verse; knattleikr victory as captain **+1**.
- **Punishments** (un-Viking conduct): cowardice before witnesses **−1 to −3 Orðstír, −1 Hamingja**, 1 níð-mark if formal; oathbreaking **−2 Orðstír, +1 Wyrd-debt, 1 níð-mark**; breaking a sumbel-vow **−3 Orðstír, +2 Wyrd-debt, 1 níð-mark, −1 Hamingja**; theft by stealth **−2 Orðstír, 1 níð-mark**; killing one who has yielded **−2 Orðstír, −1 Hamingja**; an insult unanswered **−1 Orðstír or 1 níð-mark**; miserliness at the feast-table **−1/−1**; mocking a true dream **−1 Orðstír (among hearers)**; ignoring a rightly-read dream **−1 Hamingja and disaster at full force**; insulting the landvættir **−2 Hamingja + haunting until atonement**; neglecting the ancestors a full year **−1 Hamingja, restless dreams**; ignoring a kin-claim without excuse **−1/−1**; betraying foster-kin **2 níð-marks**; raising a false níðstöng **2 níð-marks**; a proven ergi-act **1 níð-mark, −2 Orðstír, −1 Hamingja**; surrendering the óðal without a fight **−2/−1**; losing a ship by cowardice or negligence **−2/−1**; secret vengeance **1 níð-mark and the debt stands**; unsettled blood-debt at Burning **−1 Hamingja per month**; converting under pressure **−2 Orðstír (old-faith), −1 Hamingja**; revealed non-human among humans (§XXXIII): disadvantage on Persuasion/Deception, advantage on Intimidation, no Orðstír earned while known, Hamingja unstable.
- **The níðingr rule:** at **3 níð-marks**, a character becomes *níðingr* — oath worthless, killable without weregild ⚠️, Hamingja capped at 3.
- **GM's maxim:** *reward in public, punish in public, and never silently. The table is the Thing — let it witness everything, and the culture will teach itself.*

## Teaching Beat

Players learn that the culture did everything *aloud, with witnesses*: the Thing as the witnessing body made honor and shame a public ledger. Turning the table into the Thing — every reward announced, every punishment named, all before the group — teaches the honor economy better than any lecture on reputation mechanics.
