# The Norse Way — Episode 3: Hamingja (Luck That Lives)

**Video:** `videos/ep03-hamingja.mp4` · 1080×1920 · ~30s · Narration: Unnr (Rumi voice)

## Transcript

> The old ones didn't believe in luck — they believed in hamingja: luck as a living quality of your soul, rated zero to ten. Spend one point to reroll any d-twenty before the outcome lands. Grow it by courage, generosity, keeping oaths at cost. Lose it through cowardice, theft, broken vows. At zero, fortune itself turns its face away. Be generous and brave, and the universe leans toward you.

## The Rule (from HOMEBREW_CODEX.md §XVI)

- Hamingja 0–10, all PCs begin at 4; semi-secret (GM tracks openly, in-world no one announces it).
- Spend 1 before a d20 resolves to reroll and take the new result.
- Gain +1 ("your luck grows"): freely-entered battle won, open generous gift, oath kept at cost, sumbel-vow fulfilled, landvættir/dead honored, true dream heeded, praise-poem composed.
- Lose −1 ("your luck thins"): cowardice, oathbreaking, stealthy theft, killing the yielded, ignoring a true dream, insulting the landvættir, feast-table miserliness.
- At 0 — luckless: GM may force a reroll of a success once per session; disadvantage on Charisma checks to recruit or earn trust.
- At 8+ — luck-bright: allies within 30 feet gain +1 to their first death save of the day.
- Bequest: the dying may bequeath Hamingja point-for-point to kin, foster-kin, or sworn companions.

## Teaching Beat

Players stop treating luck as the dice and start treating it as *conduct* — the generous oath-keeper's rerolls grow while the selfish PC's dry up.
