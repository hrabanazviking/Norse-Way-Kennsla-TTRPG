# The Norse Way — Episode 10: The Stranger's Reception (How the World Greets the Non-Human)

**Video:** `videos/ep10-stranger.mp4` · 1080×1920 · ~30s · Narration: Unnr (Rumi voice)

## Transcript

> Player characters are human. The world is ninety-nine percent human. And when the uncanny walks into a settlement, calm acceptance does not exist. Roll one six-sided die: panic, terror-anger, dread-awe, obsessive reverence, possessive wonder, holy dread. Lawful folk react more extremely, chaotic folk less; warriors more, priests and magic-workers less — they may steady themselves for wary respect. The uncanny is never furniture. It is always an event. That is why the casting law exists: rarity is the price of wonder.

## The Rule (from HOMEBREW_CODEX.md §XXXIII)

- Whenever a **visibly or known** non-human enters a human community, roll 1d6 per group or notable individual: 1 Panic, 2 Terror-anger, 3 Dread-awe, 4 Obsessive reverence, 5 Possessive wonder, 6 Holy dread. **There is no "they take it in stride" result.**
- **Extremity score** = class weight + alignment weight: iron-minded (Fighter, Barbarian, Rogue, Ranger, Monk) **+1**; spirit-touched (Cleric, Druid, Wizard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Bard) **−1**; lawful **+2**; chaotic **−1**; neutral 0.
- Score > 0: roll (1+score) dice, keep the most extreme. Score < 0: roll (1+|score|) dice, keep the least extreme. Score 0: one roll. (Lawful fighter: 4 dice, most extreme; chaotic sorcerer: 3 dice, mildest.)
- A spirit-touched character may attempt a **DC 13 Wisdom save** for **wary respect** — no panic, no worship; the only one able to parley.
- While revealed: disadvantage on Persuasion/Deception, advantage on Intimidation, no Orðstír earned while known, Hamingja unstable at GM discretion.

## Teaching Beat

The player learns what the sagas assumed: in this world, the uncanny is never furniture. It is always an *event*. Rarity is not a restriction on fun; it is the price of wonder — and the lawful farmer's terror is not bigotry, it is cosmology.

## At an OSE Table

- The 1d6 table, extremity weights, and most/least-extreme dice are **identical** — OSE's Lawful/Neutral/Chaotic needs no conversion.
- OSE classes: iron-minded = Fighter, **Barbarian**, **Thief**, **Ranger**; spirit-touched = Cleric, **Druid**, **Magic-User**, **Illusionist**, **Bard** (OSE Advanced Fantasy).
- Wary respect: **Save vs. Spells** (instead of DC 13 Wisdom).
- While revealed: **−2 on reaction rolls** when parleying, **+2 on reaction rolls** when threatening; no Orðstír earned while known.
