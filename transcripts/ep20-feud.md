## The Rule (from HOMEBREW_CODEX.md §XXI)

- **The debt.** Every unlawful killing (outside holmgang, battle, or lawful execution) creates a **blood-debt** against the killer's *kin-group*, owed to the victim's *kin-group*. Record it: *who, of whom, when, witnessed by whom*. The debt has a price — the **weregild ladder** (§VI, `weregild.json`) — and a **temperature**: *Cool* (negotiable), *Warm* (insults exchanged, Thing demanded), *Burning* (raids, counter-killings, no talk).
- **Temperature rises** one step per month the debt goes unaddressed, or immediately upon any new insult, theft, or violence between the groups. At *Burning*, the GM runs **feud-turns**: each session, roll **1d6** — on **4+**, the feud acts (a kinsman is ambushed, a farm burned, cattle driven off). The players may be the debtors, the creditors, or caught between.
- **Settlement** (§VII): weregild **paid in full at the Thing, publicly**, cools the feud to *Settled* — both sides gain **+1 Orðstír** (honorable closure is honorable). Underpayment, late payment, or payment without the proper public forms keeps it *Warm*.
- **Vengeance taken** (blood for blood, done openly, announced): the debt is **transferred, not erased** — the killer's kin now hold the claim. Open, announced vengeance costs no níð-mark (it is lawful in the culture's eyes) but grants no Orðstír either; it is duty, not glory. **Secret** vengeance (murder by night, denial after) = **1 níð-mark + the debt stands** — the culture despised the hidden knife.
- **Letting it fester:** any PC whose kin-group holds an unsettled blood-debt at *Burning* for three months loses **1 Hamingja per month** — the feud poisons the luck-line (§XVI), as the sagas show again and again.

## Teaching Beat

The feud turns the party's violence into *politics with a ledger*. Players learn to ask, before the sword comes out, "who will own this killing tomorrow?" — which is the single most Viking question in the codex.

## At an OSE Table

- Recording the debt — who, of whom, when, witnessed by whom — is **identical**; the ledger is system-neutral.
- Temperature and its rises are **identical**: Cool → Warm → Burning, one step per unaddressed month or on any new insult, theft, or violence.
- Feud-turns: at Burning, each session roll **1d6** — on 4+ the feud acts — **identical**; OSE morale and hireling rules make the fallout bite, but the trigger is the same die.
- Settlement: weregild paid in full at the Thing, publicly, cools to Settled, both sides +1 Orðstír — **identical**; weregild paid in gp from the `weregild.json` ladder.
- Open vengeance transfers the debt (no níð-mark, no Orðstír); secret vengeance = 1 níð-mark + the debt stands — **identical**; at Burning for three months the PC loses 1 Hamingja per month.
