Lay your hand on it and speak your vow aloud: Before next Yule I shall— Keep that vow, and at the next feast the hall drinks to you: plus two honor, plus one luck. ## The Rule (from HOMEBREW_CODEX.md §XIX)

- The rite is played, not hand-waved: host calls the sumbel; minni-cups in order (gods, ancestors, honored dead, host's chosen); then the bragarfull, each warrior laying hand on the cup and speaking a vow aloud.
- Each full cup: Constitution save DC 10 + 2 per cup already drunk, or gain the poisoned condition until a long rest.
- Keeping a sumbel-vow: +2 Orðstír and +1 Hamingja, awarded at the next sumbel where the deed is recited before witnesses — the fastest honorable gain in the codex.
- Breaking a sumbel-vow: oathbreaker penalties doubled — −3 Orðstír (minimum 1), +2 Wyrd-debt, 1 níð-mark, −1 Hamingja. The hall remembers for years.
- Minni kept: drinking to your own dead with true words gains +1 Hamingja.

## Teaching Beat

The feast becomes a mechanical event, not set-dressing. Players learn to weigh their words before the cup touches their lips — a boast tonight is a binding quest tomorrow.
