# The Norse Way — Episode 15: The Seasonal Wheel (Winter Hands, Summer Sails)

**Video:** `videos/ep15-seasonal-wheel.mp4` · 1080×1920 · ~30s · Narration: Unnr (Rumi voice)

## Transcript

> The Norse year turns on two hinges. Winter: the sea closes, men come indoors — craft, feast, hold the Thing, pay their debts, learn the old arts. Summer: the sea opens — víking, trade, exploration. Raid in winter and you drown; never farm and you go hungry. The Viking Age was not pirates forever — it was farmers who sailed in summer, and every raid was weighed against the harvest left untended. Play the year the way a farmer's body knows it.

## The Rule (from HOMEBREW_CODEX.md §XII)

- Campaign time runs in **seasonal turns**: *Winter* (roughly November–March) and *Summer* (April–October). The GM advances the turn with a short **season-rite** at the table: what did each PC do all winter / all summer?
- **Winter turn** (no sailing — the sea is closed, mechanically: seafaring checks **automatically fail**): each PC chooses a winter-work — craft (make something of real value), hospitality (host feasts: spend silver, gain Orðstír), plotting (advance a feud or suit for the spring Thing), learning (train with a master: seiðr, galdr, skaldcraft — the *only* time long training happens). Unpaid debts come due (§VI); the Yule blót is held (§IV).
- **Summer turn** (the sea opens): each PC declares a summer-faring — *víking* (raid), *kaup* (trade voyage), *landnám* (exploration/settlement), or *heim* (stay and farm — honorable, and someone must). Raiding uses the seafaring rules (§XIII); farming is resolved with a single season-check (good harvest = wealth + Orðstír as a provider; bad harvest = hunger).
- **The teaching engine:** the turn structure *forces* the rhythm — players who try to raid in winter drown; players who never farm go hungry; the table learns the year the way a farmer's body knows it.

## Teaching Beat

The Viking Age stops being "pirates forever" and becomes what it was: *farmers who sailed in summer* — and every raid is weighed against the harvest left untended.

## At an OSE Table

- The two-turn year and the season-rite are **identical** — the calendar does not care what dice you roll.
- Winter: seafaring checks automatically fail in either system; winter-work (craft, hospitality, plotting, learning) is resolved at the table exactly as codex.
- Summer-faring choices (*víking*, *kaup*, *landnám*, *heim*) are system-neutral; raiding resolves through OSE's own naval and morale rules (§XIII); the farming season-check is a **roll-under the relevant ability** (or one d20 + OSE reaction/henchman-free ruling by the GM).
