## Transcript

> Flyting is war with words — formal, witnessed insult-dueling where wit and genealogy are the weapons. Best of three rounds: each exchange, you must speak a real insult aloud, in character, and every round it must cut deeper. Loki's rule: strike the live nerves — cowardice, kin-shame, ergi. The loser answers with holmgang or loses honor publicly. And if the níð-word goes unanswered by dusk, the accused is níðingr until blood or law answers it. Discipline your tongue like your sword-arm.

## The Rule (from HOMEBREW_CODEX.md §X)

- A flyting is a **contested series** (best of three exchanges): each round, both parties roll Charisma (Performance or Intimidation — attacker's choice, but the *form* must be spoken aloud by the player: a real insult, in character, before witnesses).
- **Escalation:** each round the insults must cut deeper — the GM judges whether the player actually *raised the stakes*; a repeated or weak insult is an automatic loss for the round. Loki's rule: accuse of ergi, cowardice, kin-shame — the culture's live nerves.
- **Outcome:** the loser must either **answer with holmgang** (§XI) or lose **2 Orðstír publicly**. The winner gains +1. If either party speaks *níð* (accusation of ergi/cowardice/perversion) and it goes unanswered by dusk, the accused is *níðingr* until they answer in blood or law.
- A skald (or a PC with a praise-poem ready) may enter a flyting *for* another — verse is a weapon you can lend.

## Teaching Beat

The player learns that words were *violence with rules* — and that in an honor culture, the tongue had to be as disciplined as the sword-arm.

## At an OSE Table

- The spoken form — a real insult, in character, before witnesses, cutting deeper every round — is **identical**; no dice can do that part.
- Each round: both parties roll **1d20 ≤ Charisma** (a trained speaker shifts the effective score −2 in their favor); attacker's choice of Performance/Intimidation flavor.
- Escalation: a repeated or weak insult is an automatic round loss, exactly as codex.
- Outcome: loser answers with holmgang (§XI) or loses 2 Orðstír publicly; winner +1; *níð* unanswered by dusk → *níðingr* until answered in blood or law.
