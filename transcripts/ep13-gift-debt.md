## The Rule (from HOMEBREW_CODEX.md §VI)

- Any gift of real value (GM's threshold: roughly **1+ ounce of silver**) creates a **debt-token** held by the giver against the receiver. Debt-tokens are **physical at the table** (stones, beads) — visible, like honor.
- The receiver *must* reciprocate with something of **greater value** before the next seasonal turn (§XII), or lose **1 Orðstír per unanswered token** — and the giver gains +1 (generosity witnessed). Refusing a gift outright is itself an insult (−1 Orðstír to the refuser) unless a reason is spoken before witnesses.
- **Weregild** (man-price) is the formalized gift that *ends* a feud instead of starting a friendship: gold that covers the wound. See `weregild.json` for the price ladder in silver weight (mark = 8 aurar/ounces = 24 ertogar): a free karl's life is the base unit; jarls, goðar, and kings scale upward; wounds, insults, and thefts have scheduled prices. Exact sums varied by district and law-code — the table's ladder is simplified and flagged.

## Teaching Beat

The player discovers an economy where *money is memory* — every transaction is a relationship, and wealth hoarded is wealth wasted. The modern "gold pieces in a pouch" instinct quietly dies.

## At an OSE Table

- The debt-token economy is **identical** — honor is honor in any century; tokens are stones and beads at the table either way.
- Gifts and Orðstír gains/losses are system-neutral; refusing a gift without spoken reason is −1 Orðstír in both.
- Weregild: paid in gp — the OSE economy already speaks this language; the silver-weight ladder in `weregild.json` (mark = 8 aurar) needs no conversion.
