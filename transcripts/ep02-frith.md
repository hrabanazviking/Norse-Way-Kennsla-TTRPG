# The Norse Way — Episode 2: Frith (The Eight Circles of Sacred Peace)

**Video:** `videos/ep02-frith.mp4` · 1080×1920 · ~30s · Narration: Unnr (Rumi voice)

## Transcript

> Frith isn't just peace — it's a sacred state, kept alive by how everyone behaves. Picture eight circles: your hearth, the sanctuary, your kin, your oaths, the Thing, the land itself. Break frith inside a circle — with violence, theft, or insult — and your honor drops, the wronged gain a weregild claim, and the Norns mark you with wyrd-debt: invisible ill-luck the GM can spend against you. Peace here is technology, not background.

## The Rule (from HOMEBREW_CODEX.md §II)

- Every scene sits inside one or more frith-circles; the GM names the circle aloud.
- Breach (violence, theft, insult inside a circle) is graded by sanctity — hearth and vé gravest.
- Social cost: −2 Orðstír for hearth/vé, −1 for lesser circles; the wronged gain a weregild or vengeance claim. Three breaches, or one grave breach: the community may declare you *vargr*.
- Cosmic cost: +1 Wyrd-debt per breach — the GM may spend it to impose disadvantage, described as the world turning cold.
- Mending only by public settlement: weregild at the Thing, or formal apology with gifts before witnesses.

## Teaching Beat

Peace was not a default — it was sacred technology maintained by ritual conduct. Violating it offended the cosmos, not just the victim.
