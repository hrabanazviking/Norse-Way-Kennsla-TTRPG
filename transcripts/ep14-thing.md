# The Norse Way — Episode 14: The Thing (Law Without Kings)

**Video:** `videos/ep14-thing.mp4` · 1080×1920 · ~30s · Narration: Unnr (Rumi voice)

## Transcript

> The Thing is law without kings — the assembly where free men settle blood-debts before witnesses. Four phases: the charge is announced, both parties swear with oath-helpers, the pleading begins — Insight to catch lies, Persuasion to move the crowd — and the law-speaker pronounces judgment: weregild, reconciliation, or outlawry. Full outlawry means any man may kill you, none may shelter you, and your name is struck from the community's frith. Law was procedure, and procedure was theater.

## The Rule (from HOMEBREW_CODEX.md §VII)

- A Thing session is a **structured social encounter**, run in phases:
  1. **Announcement** — the accuser states the charge before the assembly (Charisma/Performance; a well-spoken charge grants advantage later).
  2. **Oath** — both parties swear, each with oath-helpers (compurgators): the more oath-helpers of good Orðstír, the stronger the oath. An oath sworn falsely is discovered only by wyrd — but the GM notes it, and Wyrd-debt accrues (§III).
  3. **Pleading** — each side argues; witnesses speak. A skill challenge: Insight to catch lies, Persuasion to move the crowd, History to cite precedent. The *crowd's mood* is a visible tracker.
  4. **Judgment** — the law-speaker or jarl pronounces: weregild (§VI), outlawry, or reconciliation sealed with gifts and a feast.
- **Outlawry grades:** *fjörbaugsgarðr* (lesser) — exile for three years, may not be fed or sheltered at home; *skóggangr* (full) — "wolf's outlawry": killable by anyone without weregild, goods forfeit, name struck from the community's frith. A PC under full outlawry *loses all Orðstír* and gains the status *vargr*.
- **Weapons are peace-bound** at the Thing (*þingfriðr*, §II): drawing steel in the circle is a grave frith-breach — the law protects itself first.

## Teaching Beat

The player learns that "law" meant *a community watching itself decide* — and that in a world without police, procedure *was* the police. The skill challenge teaches rhetoric as a survival skill.

## At an OSE Table

- The four phases and the crowd-mood tracker are **identical** — procedure is procedure in any century.
- Announcement: well-spoken charge → **+2** on the pleading rolls; Oath: oath-helpers of good Orðstír weigh exactly as codex.
- Pleading: Insight → **roll 1d20 ≤ Wisdom**; Persuasion → **2d6 reaction roll**; History → **roll 1d20 ≤ Intelligence**.
- Outlawry: as codex §VII; additionally no hirelings will serve you and no stronghold will take you in.
- Drawing steel in the circle: frith-breach as codex §II, either system.
